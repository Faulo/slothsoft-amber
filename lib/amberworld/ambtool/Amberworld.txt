         AMBERWORLD - THE AMBERMOON OPEN SOURCE PROJECT

 The Amberworld project is an attempt to create an interpreter
 for the role-playing Amiga classic Ambermoon and games with a
    similar engine like Amberstar, Dragonflight and Albion.

   The idea behind Amberworld was greatly influenced by the
    successful ScummVM and Sarien projects, both hosted on
        Sourceforge, the ultimate Open Source network!

Dragonflight, Amberstar and Ambermoon were created by Thalion,
 once a great software company from Germany and developers of
several successful Amiga and Atari titles. All their games are
 legally available for free from many "Abandonware" sites and
the "Thalion Webshrine". Albion was created by Blue Byte, soon
 after Thalion quit business. It is still available from eBay
          but only runs on stone-aged MSDOS systems.
